# Residual changes and paid polar retries

All experiments use the original balanced B100 encoder with 8192 unknown placements, an 87-bit payload, CRC16, polar length 512, n=30,000 and IID K100 messages at 0.5 dB. DEC-35 had already shown that changing the physical numerator can restore true paths lost before the CRC. On two prior panels, frozen residuals with either the fixed or updated scalar variance failed to recover 49/76 and 7/12 newly retained IDs from the extended count-growth trajectory. This motivated a different question: can the receiver tell which previously searched pattern merits one of its next paid queries? Earlier dynamic reranking (139 to 136 misses on 64 complete continuations) concentrated gains and losses on two frames and failed its gate.

### Current activity and unsigned displacement

For fitted action a let r=y−Φa and v=σ²+max(K−|a|−1,0)/n. The actual inner LLR on a placed output is 2Ar/v. The difference from its previous query splits into a physical numerator change at the common current v and a separate rescaling of the previous signal. We multiplied current folded activity by a bounded measure of the difference between independent sign distributions induced by the two physical numerators. Its variance-only counterpart changes just the scale. The 64 finite reference cases check this sign-law statistic, not its calibration on the polar code. [Calculation and declaration](retry_design.md)

The table uses the first eligible boundary per archived frame. All arms pay the original eight SCL128 slots and have the same count of retries and the same first-visit patterns. The decision uses only previous queries, current counts and the observed residual. Each fixed batch feeds a common bank, unrestricted refit and first-copy completion. Misses are conditional batch endpoints; the complete receiver continues to process other slots.

### Direction of the update on old polar hypotheses

For an isolated active pattern with additive Gaussian noise at fixed v, let Z(L) be its signed, code-marginal likelihood ratio against noise. The identity Z(L)/Z(L₀)=E[exp((L−L₀)·s/2) | L₀, active] follows by changing the weights on each allowed sign vector s. With independent fair signs over the full cube this transport maps the old folded score exactly onto the new folded score. The prototype anchors at the old folded score and replaces the unknown old sign posterior by the weighted finite set of distinct legal CRC re-encodings of the previous inner list's payloads. A control transports the 128 raw polar leaves, including CRC failures. The true word can lie outside either pool. These words serve only to decide which pattern gets another ordinary SCL query. At h>1 or following mistaken cancellations the Gaussian single-active likelihood is itself approximate. [Derivation and gates](retry_direction.md)

On the 96 archived cases disjoint from both previous panels, 19 supplied an eligible state. Each arm spent 131 retries and 21 first visits. Magnitude, folded, legal transport and uncorrected transport produced 2/3/5/4 new true IDs, 233/232/230/231 misses after the batch, and 31 false copies each. The legal transport beat folded by one ID on each of 6506 and 6624. Neither target was in the previous re-encoded pool. The sets of *all* previously missing true IDs in the examined old corrected pools were empty. All newly found IDs nevertheless appeared in the incumbent's eventual bank. Legal transport beat uncorrected transport only on 6624, insufficient to attribute a repeatable gain specifically to CRC repair. The earlier native-SIC re-encoding oracle (197 and 390 unchanged covered copies at K50 and K100) addressed direct bank admission. Its containment result is consistent with this directional ranking mechanism.

Across 583 historical full SCL lists, every CRC-valid subset matched the archived output. Reconstructing those lists took 7.76 s separately; an implementation can collect the survivors when the original query runs. The diagnostic encoded 73,800 distinct corrected and 74,624 inner waveforms. Legal processing cost 0.956 s, compared with 2.043 s for its 152 new SCL queries. The raw source path pool would take 174,720 bytes at the largest selected boundary at 13 bytes per 103-bit leaf, before keys, residuals and object overhead. The initial launch stopped at a filename assertion; all 96 actual held-out files were frozen before case scoring. A separate readback reconciled all 96 records, 1,995 earlier LLRs, 210 distinct new SCL calls, and 184 available same-LLR archived output lists. ### Fresh complete receiver

The prospective pair used 32 new balanced seeds, 88001–88032, and the registered count-growth DEC-35 as its control. The alternative captures ordinary pre-CRC survivors while the original SCL calls run. It may reorder earlier-pattern retries in one real 105-query block, preserving the original exact-hit and first-visit slots at the decision point. Each logical slot uses the current physical residual and immediately refits on any new ID as usual. Both arms executed their own native stage, rollback, continuation and completion. A cold policy-disabled check on 6506 reproduced all 735 ordinary continuation queries. All 13 finite checks passed. Eight eligible decisions had h=1–3. Seven changed the order, moving 156 positions overall. In every changed block the original and revised queues contained the same 105 patterns. More strongly, all 735 pattern-matched calls saw the same pre-query fit, byte-identical-input digest, ordered IDs and hit status; the pre-query fit was constant throughout each block. At fixed r and v, each deterministic SCL result depends on the pattern alone, so permuting this full queue produces the same union. For these frames the observed constancy of the fit satisfies that condition. The two earlier held-out advances over folded activity occurred when h was 61 and 68, and the corresponding full IDs eventually entered the historical incumbent banks. Their contribution was earlier access to two true words under an eight-call budget. Stop the tested one-block retry policy. Whether direction can reduce the *number* of paid calls or extend eventual coverage at a high-residual state would require an explicit stopping/discovery operation and its own evidence.
